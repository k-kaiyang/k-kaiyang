Parry Everything: flow-field pathfinding

DigiPen CSD14512025C++Alpha Engine View code →

Parry Everything is a top-down roguelite where you time parries against every enemy. I made every enemy find the player through randomly generated dungeons.

Problem

Every enemy in a dungeon had to reach the player around walls and lava. The dungeon layout changes every run.

Constraint

Many enemies chase one target at the same time, so running a separate search for each one would repeat the same work.

What I changed

I built one field centred on the player that every enemy shares, in three steps:

  • Cost field: each tile gets a cost, and walls are blocked.
  • Integration field: spreading out from the player tile, each tile records its cheapest total cost to reach the player.
  • Flow field: each tile points to whichever of its 8 neighbours is cheapest, so an enemy just follows the arrow under its feet.

Enemies stop once they are close to the player. I also wrote the game's sound manager (3 audio groups, volume and mute) and a particle system that reuses a pool of particles for walking dust and lava effects.

What happened

The flow field steers all 3 enemy types (normal, explosive and boss). My parts came to 966 lines of C++ across 6 files.
