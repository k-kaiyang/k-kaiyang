Umapyoi Engine: state machine and enemy AI

Team of 6 · DigiPen CSD2401/CSD2451 · 2025 – 2026 · C++20, Lua, FMOD · View code

Umapyoi is a custom C++ 2D engine and a top-down action game where you fight Fire, Water and Wind demons. I built the system that decides what every character is doing, and wrote the demons' behaviour on top of it.

Gameplay video or GIF: demons switching between chase, attack and stunned

Problem

Gameplay was written in Lua scripts, but the engine is C++. Each enemy, the player and the boss needed separate states (idle, chase, attack, stunned, death) that switch cleanly, and someone had to connect the two languages.

Constraint

Teammates wrote the Lua scripting layer and the Entity Component System (ECS), so my system had to fit their interfaces. Pausing the game also had to freeze every character and stop its sounds.

What I changed

  • Built an FSM component and a C++ system that calls each Lua state's state_enter and update functions, and switches to the next state on request.
  • Made pause stop each character's sounds through the audio system, which I also wrote (FMOD, 32 channels, music and effects groups, and a low-pass filter that muffles everything on pause).
  • Wrote reusable Lua state templates, then 15 demon state scripts (1,207 lines of Lua), including the Water Demon's self-destruct attack.

What happened

40 player, enemy and boss state scripts run on the system in the finished game. In total I wrote 3,677 lines of C++ in the engine, credited to me in the repository's team README.
